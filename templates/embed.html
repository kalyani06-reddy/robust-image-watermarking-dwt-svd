Embed watermark

Protect a cover image with a watermark using configurable DWT–SVD settings.

Image processing
01

Choose your images

Select a cover image and the watermark to embed.

Upload a PNG, JPG, JPEG, BMP, WEBP, TIFF, or GIF image (the first frame is used for animations). If the watermark is larger than the cover can represent, the app automatically uses all DWT subbands and reduces the embedded resolution to fit. The recovered preview is shown at the original dimensions, but details removed during reduction cannot be restored.
